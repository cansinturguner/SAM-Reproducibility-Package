# Source and Decision Record — Phase 25B

- Scenario source: M. Leonardi and G. Sirbu, “ADS-B Crowd-Sensor Network and
  Two-Step Kalman Filter for GNSS and ADS-B Cyber-Attack Detection,” *Sensors*,
  21(15), 4992, 2021, DOI `10.3390/s21154992`.
- The paper supplies the position-bias and fixed-ground-spoofer scenario
  families. It does not supply a reusable attack-labeled dataset or author code
  for this SAM experiment.
- Bearing selection, evaluated offset grid, spoofer location, preservation of
  nominal residual noise, and Phase 25A decision rule are explicit SAM
  reproduction choices.
- Therefore the correct relationship is “independent attack-model adaptation,”
  not “reproduction of Leonardi–Sirbu results.”
